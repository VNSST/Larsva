How to play Kill Chess

  • Pieces move like normal chess.
  • If you can capture, you must. Pieces that are forced to capture glow red. If several captures are possible, pick any.
  • Win by losing all your pieces — or by having no legal move left.
  • The king is just a normal piece. No check, no checkmate, no castling. It can be captured.
  • Pawns can promote to a king too.
  • It’s a draw after 50 moves without a capture or pawn move, if the same position happens 3 times, or if only bishops on opposite colours are left (they can never meet).
